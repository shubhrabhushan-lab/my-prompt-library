Study

Explain a difficult topic

Act as a patient tutor. Explain [topic] for a [grade or experience level] learner. Start with a simple explanation, give one everyday example, define important terms, and finish with three short questions to check my understanding. Do not give the answers until I try.

Study

Create a revision plan

Help me make a realistic revision plan for [subject or exam]. I have [number] days and can study for [time] each day. Divide the topics into manageable sessions, include short breaks and practice questions, and leave time for revision. Ask me about any missing details first.

Writing

Improve a paragraph

Review the paragraph below for clarity, grammar and flow. Keep my original meaning and writing level. First show the improved paragraph, then list the most important changes and explain why they help. Do not add facts that I have not provided.\n\nParagraph: [paste your paragraph]

Writing

Plan an article

Help me plan an informative article about [topic] for [audience]. Suggest a clear title, a short introduction, 4–6 logical section headings, key points to research under each heading, and a useful conclusion. Mark claims that need fact-checking and do not invent sources.

Coding

Understand code

Explain what this [language] code does, step by step, for someone with [experience level]. Describe the purpose of each important section, define unfamiliar terms, and give a small example input and output. If you spot a possible issue, explain it without changing the code yet.\n\nCode: [paste code]

Coding

Debug a programming error

Help me investigate this [language or tool] error. Explain what the error message means, identify likely causes from the code and context I provide, and suggest a minimal correction. Explain why the correction works and mention how I can test it. Do not assume files or details I have not shared.\n\nError and code: [paste here]

Research

Build a research outline

Help me investigate [research question]. Break it into smaller questions, identify what kinds of reliable sources would help, suggest neutral search phrases, and create an outline for recording findings. Separate established facts from questions that still need evidence.

Research

Compare two ideas

Compare [option A] and [option B] for [specific purpose]. Use criteria that matter for this purpose, explain the trade-offs in a table, identify information that is missing or uncertain, and finish with questions I should answer before choosing. Do not invent specifications.

Productivity

Break down a project

Turn this project goal into a practical task plan: [goal]. Break it into milestones and small next actions, estimate which tasks depend on others, and suggest a simple way to track progress. Keep the plan realistic for my available time: [available time].

Productivity

Prepare for a presentation

Help me prepare a [length]-minute presentation about [topic] for [audience]. Create a clear opening, a logical sequence of key points, suggestions for simple visuals, and a concise closing. Use language suitable for the audience and flag facts I should verify.

Everyday

Make a decision checklist

Help me make a balanced checklist for deciding whether to [decision]. Include benefits, drawbacks, costs or effort, possible risks, alternatives, and questions to ask someone knowledgeable. Do not decide for me; help me compare the factors clearly.

Learning

Learn through practice

Teach me the basics of [skill] through a short beginner lesson. Explain one idea at a time, show a simple example, then give me one practice task. Wait for my attempt before giving feedback, and adapt the next task to what I found difficult.

How to get better results from ChatGPT

  1. State your goal: explain what you want ChatGPT to help you do.
  2. Add context: include the audience, level, subject or constraints that matter.
  3. Specify the format: ask for steps, a table, examples or a short explanation.
  4. Refine the result: ask follow-up questions if the first response is too broad.
  5. Check important facts: verify key information using reliable sources.

Frequently asked questions

Are these ChatGPT prompts free to use?

Yes. You can copy and adapt the examples on this page. You may need an account or an available plan to use a particular AI service.

How do I use a ChatGPT prompt?

Choose a prompt, copy it, replace the bracketed parts with details about your task, and paste it into ChatGPT. Review the answer and ask follow-up questions when needed.

Can I edit these prompts?

Yes. These are starting points. Adjust the context, instructions and requested output to match your needs.

Are AI responses always correct?

No. AI tools can make mistakes or provide outdated information. Check important facts, calculations and sources independently.
